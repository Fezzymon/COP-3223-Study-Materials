- The "Dot" operator (.)
		- Used to access a structure variable
		- Syntax: (struct's_variable_name).(member's_name)
		- Ex. p.year = 2000; strcpy(p.name, "John Doe");
- The "Arrow" operator (->)
	- Used when using a pointer-TO-the structure and accessing its member 
	- Equivalent to: (*p).member
		- Use as: p->member
- Arrays-of-Structures
	- Used as databases of multiple structures, Ex. students and their invisual databases
	- Syntax: parent_struct_name child_struct_name[5];
		- child_struct_name[0].member;
